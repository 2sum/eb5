Timelines & Law

The core basis of WoM is unreasonable delay. For EB5 application there has been an unwritten guideline that folks follow depending on their risk profile and this keeps changing with the times.

The law (RIA) has put some expectations for processing timelines for 526 applications. It expects TEA applications to be processed within 120 days. TEA includes both Rural and Urban.

There are no guidelines baked into the law for 485. There are some general APA guidelines which state that a reasonable time-frame for 485 to be processed is 181 days.

Vast majority of attorneys recommend filing after a minimum of 6 months. Until middle of 2025, attorneys were VERY conservative (8-12 months).

This changed around July 2025 (Q4 FY2025) when folks filed pro-se WoMs with aggressive timelines. One successful case was filed at just 110 days.

Current (late 2025/early 2026) guidelines for pro-se:

  • Rural: Aggressive 120 days, reasonable 180 days
  • HUA: Aggressive 180 days, reasonable 240 days

Note: Recommended wait is higher for HUA likely because the law mentions priority for rural but not explicitly for HUA, impacting human psychology/reasonableness.

Pro-Se Filing

Pro-se means representing yourself without an attorney. "Pro-se WoM" means filing the lawsuit by yourself.

Primary reasons include cost and control. You manage the case and interactions with opposing counsel directly.

However, it comes at the expense of your time. If you prefer "peace of mind," a professional might be better as pro-se requires you to stay "plugged in."

  • Pro-se: $405 court fees + mailing. Inexpensive help (Fiverr $70-$500 or Aninda ~$500). Note: Read on UPL before seeking non-attorney help.
  • Attorney Groups: $3k to $3.5k.
  • Attorney Solo: $8k to $12k.
Group vs Solo & Attorney Roles

Effectiveness hierarchy (1 is most effective):

  1. Solo
  2. Small groups (2 to 3)
  3. Big groups (4 or more)

WoM is a different expertise. If you ask Anahita, she will refer to Matt Galati. If you are with KLDP, Niral may offer services. There is close to zero advantage in using your filing attorney for a WoM; they are separate legal matters.

You do not necessarily need to inform your filing attorney of a WoM. If you get an RFE, their responsibility to respond remains the same regardless of the lawsuit.

Advanced Scenarios (2026 Trends)

In Q4 2025, 526 approvals shrunk to 4 months. Many found themselves with 526 approved but 485 stuck. Pro-se filers successfully filed "485-only" cases at the 4-5 month mark. Best strategy: Plan to file 485+526 together, but if 526 clears, switch to 485-only.

If retrogression hits, you lose the window to file a WoM. However, filing too early risks a Motion to Dismiss (MTD) with prejudice. If dismissed with prejudice, you'll have a tough time filing again for the same relief. Balancing this risk is the toughest part of the strategy.

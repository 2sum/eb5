"Pro-se" means representing yourself without an attorney.

Advantages:
• Cost: Court fees are ~$405 vs. $8k-$12k for a solo attorney.
• Control: You manage the interaction with opposing counsel directly.
• Efficiency: In 2025/2026, many pro-se filers found success with aggressive timelines (some as early as 110 days).
